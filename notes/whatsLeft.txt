ABOUT HOW TO SEND MESSAGES:
- to make a message you just grab its constructor
  then plug in the needed parameters then right after the 
  constructor you call the .toMessageFormat() method and 
  assign it to a variable
- you then call the sendToPeer() method of the transport object
  and pass the variable as a parameter
- sample code:
  	message = HandshakeRequest().toMessageFormat()
  	self.transport.sendToPeer(message)

ABOUT WHEN SENDING MESSAGES:
- each time you send the messages seen in section 4
  of the specs, the receiver should send you an Acknowledgement
- Acknowledgement is special since we need to send these after 
  all the other messages
- as a general rule, the receiver can't send what they want to send
  until they reply to the sender with an Acknowledge message
- when you as a sender don't receive an Acknowledgement within 
  500 milliseconds, you can resend your message 2 more times 
  (so a total of 3 sends)
- if the resends don't give you an acknowledge the entire program should end
